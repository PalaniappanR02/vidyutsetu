  • Part 1: AC static direct connected meters

IEC 61968-9 (CIM for distribution)

          

            Vendor-neutral back-office message schemas: meter measurements become MeterReadings, grid alarms become EndDeviceEvents.
          

          
    
            
  • ReadingQuality: valid, missing, estimated, suspect
    • 
            
  • EndDeviceEvent: tamper, power quality, outage codes
    • 
            
  • IntervalBlocks: time-series telemetry payloads
    • 
          

        

      

      

        The workbench validates and normalizes payloads in software. It does not emulate RF mesh, cellular or optical probe protocols, and it does not certify formal conformance.
